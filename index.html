Common questions

The camera isn't counting my punches well

  • Put the phone at face height, about 1.5 m (5 ft) away, on a shelf or a stack of books.
  • You should see yourself from head to hips.
  • Have good light in front of you, not behind you.
  • Punch at the phone, as if it were your opponent's chin.

Is my video recorded or uploaded?

No. Punch tracking runs on your phone and no video ever leaves it. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Southpaw → Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

I got a new phone. How do I get my subscription back?

On the paywall, tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Profile → Delete account and data. This deletes your server account (nickname, XP, records, duels) and everything stored on your phone.

Is this medical advice?

No. Southpaw gives general fitness information. If something hurts, stop and see a professional.
