Southpaw Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Southpaw: AI Boxing Coach ("Southpaw") is a boxing training app for iPhone. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain.

Who we are

Southpaw is made by Jülide Çokşen, an independent developer based in Turkey ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us at the email address at the end of this policy.

The short version

  • Your camera video never leaves your phone. Punch tracking runs on your device. We never record, upload or store your video.
  • Videos you pick for a form check stay on your phone too. The phone reads them; the video itself is never uploaded.
  • Form check photos only with a separate permission. If you allow it, up to 16 still photos of you from a form check round are sent to the AI to spot technique faults. They are not stored on our server.
  • No sign-up is needed. The app makes an anonymous account so leaderboards and 1v1 work. Signing in with Apple is optional.
  • AI coaching only with your permission. Before anything is sent to the AI, the app asks you. If you say no, your plan is built on your phone instead.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't use it for ads. We don't use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and we don't track you across other apps.

What we collect and why

1. Kept only on your phone

Your onboarding answers (experience, goals, stance, schedule, equipment, injuries, age, height, weight, goal weight), your training plan, workout history, punch statistics, form check results and weigh-ins are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

2. Your account (on our server)

To run leaderboards and 1v1 duels we keep, on our server (Supabase):

  • an anonymous account ID, created automatically
  • your nickname (random by default, you can change it)
  • your weekly XP, Sprint records and 1v1 duel results (score, rating)
  • a daily count of AI requests, to keep usage fair
  • if you choose Sign in with Apple: the Apple identifier and email address Apple shares with us (you can hide your email using Apple's "Hide My Email")

Other users can see your nickname, XP, Sprint records and duel scores on the leaderboards. They can't see anything else.

3. AI coaching (only with your permission; sent for an answer, not stored)

Some features use AI (OpenAI) through our server. The app asks for your permission before the first one. You can say no, or turn it off later in Profile; your plan is then built on your phone, and the AI-only features (Ask your coach, form check feedback) are switched off. With your permission, the following is sent:

  • Your plan: your onboarding answers (including age, height, weight, goals and injuries) are sent once to personalise your training plan.
  • Form check report: the measured numbers from your round (punch counts, timing, guard and head-movement statistics) and your experience, stance and injuries.
  • Ask your coach: the question you pick and a summary of your training numbers (including your weight progress if you log your weight).

No video, name or email is ever sent to the AI, and no photo unless you allow form check photos (below). Our server does not store these requests. OpenAI processes them under its API terms and does not use API data to train its models.

3b. Form check photos (only with a separate permission)

Form check can give feedback on your technique from photos. The first time, the app asks you separately whether it may send them; you can say no and still use the rest of the app, and you can turn it off later in Profile → Settings → Privacy. With your permission:

  • The phone picks up to 16 still photos from your form check round (the moments you punch) and sends them, through our server, to OpenAI. At most once a week.
  • The AI only answers which visible faults it sees (for example the other hand leaving your face) and in which photos. It is told not to identify anyone.
  • Our server does not store the photos; it passes them on and returns the answer. OpenAI processes them under its API terms and does not use API data to train its models.
  • A copy of the photos stays on your phone so the report can show them, for your two newest reports only. They are not included in iCloud backup and are deleted with Delete account and data.

The photos show you and whatever is behind you, so we suggest a plain background.

4. App usage analytics

We use Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand how the app is used, for example which screens people visit, where they stop during setup, and which drills are finished. It receives screen names, simple events (such as "drill finished" with the mode, seconds and punch count), your anonymous account ID, and basic device information (model, iOS version, country). It does not receive your video, weight, answers, nickname or email, and it does not use the advertising identifier. You can turn usage stats off at any time in Profile.

4b. Crash reports

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) sends us a crash report so we can fix the problem. It contains the technical details of the crash (which part of the code failed), basic device information (model, iOS version) and your anonymous account ID. It does not contain your video, weight, answers, nickname or email.

5. Subscriptions

Purchases are made through Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. It receives your anonymous account ID and the purchase details Apple provides (product, dates, status). We never see your payment card details.

6. Reminders

Training reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification data is sent to us.

Why we're allowed to use it (UK and EU users)

  • To provide the app (your account, leaderboards, duels, subscriptions): necessary to provide the service you asked for.
  • AI coaching, including any health information you give (injuries, weight): your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Profile.
  • Form check photos: your explicit consent, asked separately, which you can withdraw at any time in Profile.
  • Usage analytics: our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app, with an easy way to turn it off in Profile.
  • Crash reports: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working.

Where your data is processed

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries outside the UK and EU. Where this happens, they use recognised safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Data on your phone: until you delete the app or use Delete account and data.
  • Form check photos: not kept on our server; on your phone only for your two newest reports.
  • Account data on our server: until you delete your account.
  • Analytics: Firebase keeps event data for up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports: Crashlytics keeps them for 90 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Profile → Delete account and data deletes your server account (nickname, XP, records, duels) and everything stored on your phone.
  • Camera: you can turn camera access off in iOS Settings. Timer-only workouts still work.
  • AI and photos: you can turn AI coaching and form check photos off at any time in Profile → Settings → Privacy.
  • Questions or requests (access, correction, deletion): email us at the address below.

UK and EU users can ask for a copy of their data, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict its use, take it to another service, and withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect what was done before). You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). We'd appreciate the chance to help first.

US users: we do not sell or share your personal information for targeted advertising. Depending on your state (for example California) you may have the right to know, correct and delete your data; email us and we'll help.

Children

Southpaw is for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from younger children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

Health and safety

Southpaw gives general fitness information, not medical advice. Injury answers are used only to leave out exercises that could hurt you.

Service providers

  • OpenAI (AI coaching text, form check photo feedback)
  • Google Firebase (app analytics, crash reports)
  • RevenueCat (subscription status)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases)

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
